A people counter for the door

Count who comes in and who leaves, see how many people are inside right now, and get an alert when you reach capacity. For events, shops, classrooms and anywhere with a door and a limit.

Guide for the Counter app on Android. Works offline, with no account.

What you need

  • The free Counter app for Android.
  • The capacity of the room, if you want an alert.

Setup A: one counter for how many are inside

The quickest setup, for a single door.

  1. Tap the + icon in the top bar of the home screen and create a counter called "Inside".
  2. Set the minimum value to 0 and the maximum value to the capacity of the room.
  3. Tap + when someone comes in and − when someone leaves. With the counter open, the volume buttons do the same: volume up for in, volume down for out.

To be warned at capacity, add a rule under What it does on its own: event Reaches its maximum, action Send notification, text "At capacity".

Setup B: entries and exits counted separately

Use this when you also want the total number of visitors, not only how many are inside now.

  1. Create two counters, "In" and "Out".
  2. Open tap zones from the top bar of the home screen and add both. The screen splits in two big buttons, one per counter, and stays on: tap without looking.
  3. Create a calculated view called "Inside" with a formula that subtracts Out from In. Formulas reference counters by ID: if In is counter 1 and Out is counter 2, the formula is c1 - c2.

"In" is the number of visitors of the day, "Inside" updates on its own after every tap.

Start from zero every day

Add a rule on each counter, choose At a time, then Every day at with the action Reset counter. Pick an hour when the place is closed. On Android 13 and later, time rules need the exact alarm permission.

See when people arrive

Every tap is saved with its time. The counter's Overview shows the busiest hours and a trend by day, week or month, and its history can be exported as CSV for a report.

Share the live count: add a rule Changes with the action Telegram Message and the text ${counter.title}: ${counter.value} to post every change to a staff group (see the Telegram guide), or use a webhook to Google Sheets for a shared log.

Things you can count the same way

  • Guests at an event against the size of the guest list.
  • Passengers boarding a bus or a boat.
  • Cars in a car park, with an alert when it is full.
