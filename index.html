What it does

More than a counter

Everything a tally counter, habit counter and goal tracker should do. Then it keeps going: automations, statistics, widgets, and integrations with your smart home.

01 · AUTOMATE

If it reaches 8,
do something

Build if-this-then-that rules with no code. Trigger on a value (reached, above, below, multiple of, even, odd, prime, palindrome, min, max) or on a schedule (every day, weekly, monthly, every N minutes). Then notify, reset, chain counters, message Telegram, fire a webhook or wake up Tasker.

Integrations

Connect everything

Your counters don't live in isolation. They talk to your smart home, your automation apps, your web services, and physical NFC tags.

Webhooks

Fire HTTP requests (GET, POST, PUT, PATCH, DELETE) with custom headers, authentication, JSON payloads and template variables. Connect to Home Assistant, Google Sheets, Zapier, Make, or any REST API.

Home AssistantGoogle SheetsZapierAny REST API

Broadcast intents

Send Android broadcast intents that Tasker, Automate and MacroDroid can catch, and let them control your counters in return. Turn any count into a trigger for phone automations: Bluetooth, SMS, apps, Do Not Disturb.

TaskerAutomateMacroDroidBoth directions

NFC tags

Write counter actions to physical NFC tags. Tap your phone on a sticker to increment, decrement or reset a counter, no app opening needed. Stick them on your water bottle, gym bag, or front door.
